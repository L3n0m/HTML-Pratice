Patatas et rum

Introduced in Italy in the mid 16 century by the catholic priest Narcisus romanus the sixth, this dish offer the finest of italian cuisine, with lots of flavor and all of the most acclaimed italian traditional seasonings.

Ingredients

  • 4 big onions
  • 2 sweet potatoes
  • 1 & 1/2 cloves of garlic
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • 50ml of rum (prefer aged rum for a better experience)

Instructions

  1. Start by micing the garlic and icing the onions
  2. Next cut the potatoes (commonly refered as patatas in many latine languages) just enough to you can open them slighly
  3. Stuff the potatoes with the minced garlic and the onions, add a pinch of salt and the black pepper
  4. Add to a tray and drizzle it with the rum, then bake till brown and voilá!
